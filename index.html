Equation notation

  • Cs: Source RGB color of the incoming fragment
  • Cd: Destination RGB color already stored in the framebuffer
  • As: Source alpha(opacity). Ranging from 0 to 1
  • Cout: Resulting RGB color after blending

WebGL blend factors and operation

  • SRC_ALPHA Multiply by source opacity(As)
  • ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA Multiply by 1 minus source opacity(1 - As)
  • ONE: Retain the full color contribution
  • FUNC_ADD Add the weighted source and destination colors
